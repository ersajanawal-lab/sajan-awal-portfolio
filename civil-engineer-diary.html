About Civil Engineer Diary

My purpose is to bridge gaps between technical knowledge, site practice, and project management, helping construction professionals understand the work and ask better questions before acting.

My experience includes residential drawings and estimates, construction inspections, site engineering at Dharmashala, and planning, coordination, supervision, and documentation at Dharahara. I am continuing my learning through a Master of Construction Management at CQUniversity in Sydney.

Each developed story will explain the situation, the gap, my responsibility and actions, the supported outcome, and what another professional should clarify before acting.

These reflections support professional learning. They do not replace approved drawings, project specifications, site procedures, project-specific design and review, or appropriately qualified professional advice.
